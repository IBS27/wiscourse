Privacy policy

Effective September 9, 2026

wiscourse is an independent interface for Canvas, operated by Srinivas I B. This policy describes how wiscourse handles information when you use wiscourse.com.

Account and Google sign-in

Clerk provides account authentication. If you sign in with Google, Clerk receives your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile picture to create and authenticate your account. wiscourse uses your account identifier to associate your data with your account and displays your profile information in the account menu.

Google sign-in requests only basic identity information. It does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or Google Calendar. Google identity information is not used for advertising or sent to the AI course-map service. You can revoke the Google connection in your Google Account settings.

Canvas and information you enter

If you connect Canvas, wiscourse receives the Canvas instance address and access token you provide. The token is encrypted before storage and used on the server to access your Canvas account. Synced information can include your Canvas name and identifier, courses, assignments, deadlines, submission status, grades and feedback, announcements, discussions, course pages, modules, file information, and instructor information.

wiscourse stores synced data to display and search your courses and keep your dashboard updated. It also stores tasks, planning choices, read status, and other preferences you enter. Browser storage is used for preferences such as appearance, and authentication uses cookies and related session storage.

AI course maps

When you enable a course map, relevant course content is sent to OpenAI to interpret the course structure. This can include syllabuses, assignment descriptions, course pages, and text extracted from course documents. The generated map and records of the generation, including prompts and responses, are stored in Convex. Course content can itself contain personal information. Canvas access tokens are not included in model prompts.

Service providers

wiscourse uses Vercel to host the website, Clerk to manage accounts and authentication, Convex to store and process application data, and OpenAI for AI course maps. These providers process the information needed to supply their services and may collect technical information such as IP addresses, request details, and logs. Connecting Canvas also sends authenticated requests to your Canvas provider.

wiscourse does not sell personal information or use Google user data for targeted advertising. Google user data is used for the sign-in and account features described above. wiscourse's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Account information, synced Canvas data, tasks, and generated course maps remain stored until removed. Disconnecting Canvas deletes the stored Canvas credential and disables course-map updates; it does not delete previously synced data or your tasks. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete your wiscourse data.

To request access to or deletion of your stored information, email ibsrinivas27@gmail.com from your account email address. Deleted information may remain in service-provider backups and operational logs according to their retention processes.

Security, changes, and contact

wiscourse uses HTTPS, authenticated access to application data, and encryption for stored Canvas tokens. No system can guarantee complete security.

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes to how Google user data is used will require notice and consent before that new use.

For privacy questions, contact Srinivas I B at ibsrinivas27@gmail.com.
