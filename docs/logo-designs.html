Wordmark typeface

The wordmark will be converted to SVG outlines, so it renders the same on every machine. That means picking one typeface and freezing it. Every tile below uses the same weight (600) and the same tracking (−3.5%); the tracking gets tuned to whichever one wins. The first tile is the reference only — it is what you have been looking at, and it is the one option that cannot ship.

Apex balance

The mark is geometrically symmetric — measured on a real screenshot, both halves span 13 px wide and y38–53, and all three peaks sit on the same row. What is left is optical, and only in dark mode: pure white blooms against a dark background and red does not, so the white half reads heavier and the red half looks like it sits higher. In light mode the ink is near-black, so there is nothing to correct. Each option below is shown large, then at 20 px and 16 px, on both backgrounds.

Notes

  • Colour. Red is UW–Madison Badger Red #C5050C. On dark backgrounds it moves to #FF4B54 for contrast. The rest of each mark is text colour, so the logo inherits the theme and never needs a second file.
  • Best for a favicon. 2, 3, 5, 8, 10 and 12 stay clear at 16 px. 7 (Term) has the most detail and is the weakest small.
  • Best fit to the product idea. 4 (Mirror) and 12 (Sync Layers) say "local copy of Canvas". 1, 2 and 9 say "fast". 10 says "Wisconsin" the loudest.
  • Chosen. Wordmark 15 (Split — wisc) plus mark 2 (Fast Forward), inverted. Red leads on the left, so the mark reads left to right into the red wisc of the word. See "Selected direction" above.
  • Pairing rule. Use the mark alone for the icon, favicon and any square slot. Use the wordmark alone in the app header. Use the full lockup only where there is room for both — marketing pages, the sign-in screen, the README.
  • Dark-mode ink: #e8e8ec, not white — see Apex balance. The mark is geometrically symmetric; the correction is purely optical, and it applies to the logo only. Text stays at full --foreground.
  • Typeface: B, Inter SemiBold at −3.5% tracking, converted to outlines. The logo is no longer live text, so it is identical on every platform and needs no webfont.
  • Shipped. public/favicon.svg and src/components/logo.tsx (LogoMark, Wordmark, Logo), wired into index.html, src/index.css (the --brand token) and the app shell. App-icon PNGs and a web manifest are deliberately not included — they are only needed for a home-screen or PWA install, and can be added later without changing any of this.
