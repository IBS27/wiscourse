Canvas and wiscourse, side by side.

In Canvas

  1. Every page waits on Canvas before it shows anything.
  2. Assignments live inside each course.
  3. Grades are a page per course.
  4. Announcements are a page per course.

In wiscourse

  1. Pages read from a synced copy and open at once.
  2. One agenda across every course, with your own tasks in it.
  3. What-if grades across the whole term, once they are posted.
  4. One Inbox, in order, with read state.

Sign in. Connect Canvas once.

The first sync starts right away. Nothing is written back to Canvas unless you submit something.
