Why it is fast

Every click in Canvas is a round trip to Canvas. The course list, the assignment, the file, the grade: each one is loaded again, from scratch, while you wait.

wiscourse does the waiting for you. It keeps a copy of your courses in its own database and refreshes that copy in the background, a few minutes apart. The screens you use read only from the copy. Nothing on the page is waiting on Canvas.

So pages open the moment you ask for them, and they update on their own when something changes. Assignments from every course land in one agenda, with the dates you planned next to the dates that are due.
